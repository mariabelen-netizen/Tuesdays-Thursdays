2 · Rehearsal

Writer's check

You're the writer. Check your ending before the show.

  1. Get your ending ready: on paper or on the computer.
    No ending yet? Tell it to me first. Then write 3 or 4 lines.
  2. Underline every verb.
  3. For each verb, ask the four questions.

Past and future verbs? Not today. Leave them.

Backstage timer

0:00
1Is it a stage direction?
(what the actor does)
She opens the box.
He looks at Mia.
he / she + verb + s
2Is the character talking about now or this week?
I'm looking for my bag.
We're planning a surprise.
am / is / are + -ing
3Is it a habit?
(every day, usually, always)
She always sits by the window.
present simple
4Is it a feeling or a thought?
(know, want, need, like)
I know! (not: I'm knowing)
no -ing
